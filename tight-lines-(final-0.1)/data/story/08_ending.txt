=== ending_street | scene: overworld_spawn | requires: quest_deep_giants, unlock_ending_home
@john
Same street. Same jeepneys.
It looked so different when I left. Now it feels like I've been gone for years.

I walked past Marikina River every day and never noticed it.
Now I know exactly what could be in there.
!objective Go home.


=== ending_hallway | scene: overworld_house_1 | requires: ending_street
@john
Home.

!open_ui photo_lolo
@john
Hi, Lolo.
I used to walk past you every day and never stop.

!box parchment
@john:sad
(quietly) Lolo...
I did it. I filled the pages.

Not all of them, and not the whole world. But the Philippines.
Marikina River, Imus, Kawit, Noveleta, the mangroves, and Fortune Island.

I caught fish you never got to see. I wish you could have been there.

When I found your diary, I was stressed and tired, and I just wanted to get away from my desk.
I didn't know it would change everything.

You wrote that you regretted not fishing here.
I read that and I couldn't stop thinking about it.

So I went.

I understand now why you smiled so much in this photo.
It's not about the size of the fish.

It's the waiting, and the not knowing,
and the moment when the line pulls and everything else goes quiet.

You gave me that, even without meaning to.

You said maybe someone else would pick up the rod one day.
I did, Lolo. I picked it up.

And I'm not going to put it down.
Thank you for the dream. I'll keep it going.

I love you, Lolo.

!wait 0.8
@diary
"Well done, apo. Now keep fishing."

@john:happy
(softly) I will.

!box normal
!flag story_completed
!open_ui credits
