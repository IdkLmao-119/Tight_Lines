STYLIZED NATURE - FREE SAMPLER - 16 game-ready low poly models
=====================================================================

Free models from the Stylized Nature series - the same quality as the paid packs:
LOD0 + LOD1 meshes, convex collision meshes (UCX_), physics presets (mass stored in the FBX)
and a foliage wind mask in vertex colour R.

CONTENTS
  FBX/       16 models for Unity / Unreal (+ the 3 palette textures)
  GLB/       16 models for Godot, Three.js, web and Blender (texture embedded)
  Textures/  NaturePalette.png, NaturePalette2.png, NaturePalette3.png (one small palette per series pack)
  Unity/     Editor/StylizedNatureFreeImporter.cs - automatic colliders, physics and LOD groups
  Previews~/ a picture of every model
  catalog.json  size, triangles, physics type and which paid pack each model comes from

UNITY: copy the folder "StylizedNaturePack_FreeSampler" into Assets (keep the name).
GODOT 4: drag the GLB files in - collision shapes and LODs are created on import.
UNREAL: import the FBX files (UCX_ collision naming).

13,252 triangles for all 16 models (LOD0), real-world scale in metres, pivot at the base.

FOLDERS ENDING IN ~
  Unity skips Source~ and Previews~ (that is what the ~ means), so your project stays small and
  Unity never needs Blender. Godot skips them too (they hold a .gdignore file).
  Open them from your file explorer whenever you want them.
